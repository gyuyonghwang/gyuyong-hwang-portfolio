Project 1

Financial Spreading & Entity Matching Unit

Background

Spreading financial statements involves transforming raw financial data into a structured format that facilitates detailed analysis and risk assessment. The existing workflow requires multiple external applications to view, edit, and update financial statements, with part of the workflow being done offline. This leads to inefficiencies, time-consuming, and prone to errors, data inconsistencies, and user frustration. Users expressed a need for a single application on GCRS to streamline these critical tasks.

Role

As the sole UX designer, I created a financial spreading application from scratch, collaborating closely with users, business analysts, and developers. Closely collaborated with users from the research phase through development.
Worked closely with Data Scientists to visualise/translate data into visuals that users can easily understand. I carried out user interviews to gather requirements and built complex prototypes to conduct usability tests, addressing key pain points. Additionally, I worked closely with data scientists to automate parts of the workflow, streamlining the overall process and enhancing efficiency.

Key Achivements

Reuseabe

Provided a generic interface that works on all Global Credit Risk Applications and top of any Risk Finance Technology platforms.

Automation

Reduced manual efforts required to deal with unmatched entities, thus reducing the cost of the business process.
‍

Streamline

Streamlined the Financial Spreading Process by integrating offline workflow into the application ensuring data integrity

Operational Effiency

The application drastically shortens the user journey, thus saving time and effort for hundreds of users.

Project 2

Environmental, Social & Governance Dashboards

Background

ESG factors are increasingly recognised as significant risks that can affect the creditworthiness of borrowers. For banks, incorporating these risks into their risk management processes is essential. The ESG project equips Credit Officers with vital tools to assess and manage these risks effectively which involves understanding sector dynamics and long-term trends. These dashboards enable Credit Officers to analyse the impact of ESG factors on a company's credit profile and help identify portfolio risks.

Role

I led the UX/UI design of the ESG Program, overseeing the entire design process from start to finish. I collaborated closely with external SMEs from Accenture and in-house developers to enhance the decision-making process for Credit Officers by delivering comprehensive ESG information on GCRS.  So far, I have designed the Transition Risk Dashboard, Nature Risk Dashboard, and Net Zero Pathway Dashboard.

Key Achivements

Regulatory Compliance

Successfully met evolving ESG regulatory compliance and standards in a timely manner, avoiding fines and maintaining a good business reputation.

Workflow Integration

Identified the most relevant location for ESG information within GCRS, ensuring seamless integration with existing workflows.

Reusable Components

Added new charts to the GCRS component library for future projects, saving development costs and time.

Operational Effiency

Eliminated the offline workflow process by integrating ESG information directly into GCRS, removing the need for users to access external applications to view ESG data.

Data Visualisation

Implemented top-tier graphs and charts to simplify complex data, enabling Credit Officers to quickly grasp key information. This improved decision-making and was well received by stakeholders.

Project 3

Hedge Fund Dashboard

Background

GCRS is expanding its user base, and Hedge Fund Managers expressed interest in a dashboard providing a real-time view of key performance indicators and metrics essential for managing hedge fund operations. The Credit Risk Technology team seized the opportunity to design a dashboard that meets these needs.

Role

I collaborated with the Head of UX/UI, users, and developers to design a new dashboard with unique interactions not previously available in GCRS. My responsibilities included interaction and UI design, where I created high-fidelity prototypes to communicate complex interactions, aiding user understanding and reducing development time. Prototypes were also used for usability testing, incorporating direct user feedback to ensure the dashboard met real-world needs.

Key Achivements

Modular Design

Designed a flexible, modular dashboard that serves as a foundation for future dashboards, reducing development costs and time.

Scalability

Focused on creating reusable and scalable components, which have been adapted across multiple dashboards, such as the Portfolio Overview and ESG Dashboard.

Collaboration

Added new charts to the GCRS component library for future projects, saving development costs and time.

Project 4

Italian Small to Medium Enterprise

Background

ISME is an orchestration system tightly integrated with 30+ Italian local systems, allowing Credit Officers to conduct Rating Analysis and Credit Approval for Italian clients. With Adobe ending support for Flash, the existing ISME framework (Flex) was no longer viable, posing significant operational risks. The business decided to build and redesign the platform from the ground up using the latest technology.

Role

I led the UX/UI design for the new ISME platform, overseeing and guiding two UX designers. I collaborated closely with users, business analysts, and developers. Instead of merely migrating the old design, I undertook a complete redesign to improve usability and findability, addressing the pain points and unmet needs of over 200 Credit Officers in Italy.

Key Achivements

Operational Efficiency

Boosted operational efficiency for over 200 ISME Credit Officers by implementing bulk actions, significantly reducing repetitive tasks.

Foundation for Growth

Established the groundwork for the new ISME platform, facilitating the development of new applications and workflows.

Design Audit

Conducted a design audit of the old platform, identifying and removing redundant features, which saved development costs.

Innovative Design

Experimented with new design patterns, such as window splitters, advanced search, and bulk updates, enhancing user efficiency and productivity.

Workflow Optimisation

Streamlined the workflow by optimising the layout to ensure a natural flow, preventing users from backtracking.

Timely Delivery

Successfully delivered designs on time, ensuring the new platform was ready for use once the old platform was decommissioned, avoiding risks and ensuring a smooth transition.
