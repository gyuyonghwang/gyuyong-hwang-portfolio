Background

Gamesys had great success in online casino products over the years. During one of the market research, the business learned that most online casino players are either sport betting punters or showed a keen interest in sports betting. Sports betting is a multi-billion pounds industry in Europe alone and continues to grow. The business saw the opportunity and has decided to invest in creating a Virgin branded sports betting product. As a strategic move, they've decided to diversify their portfolio by investing in the creation of a sports betting product under the reputable Virgin brand.

Role & Responsibility

I led a team of two UX designers and worked closely with the Director of Sports, product owners, developers, and UI designers. I was responsible for the user experience of both Android and iOS applications, as well as a responsive website for desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Define

Workshop

The project commenced with a comprehensive week-long workshop involving the Director of Sports and Product Owner. Our primary objective was to determine the business requirements and understanding the vision for the product. I had the privilege of working intimately with them from the onset, which significantly broadened my understanding of sportsbooks. Despite approaching the project with limited familiarity in this field, the workshop was of tremendous value to me.

Key Takeaways

Prioritise the user's ease of use on mobile devices by ensuring that all critical and frequently used interactions are accessible within thumb's reach.

Stay ahead of the curve by exploring the latest trends in mobile navigation and gestures, such as dragging elements, swipes, and long presses.

Challenge traditional navigation and interactions, while aiming to provide the most efficient browsing experience for the user.

To streamline development time without compromising the user experience, reuse components across different devices.

Enhance user engagement by providing an option to track a sports event while browsing other parts of the app, with a feature to quickly return to it when desired.

Cross-sell casino products in a non-disruptive way, without having to navigate bettors out from the sportsbook application. Also, consider enabling users to follow a sports event while engaging with a casino game.

Feature Priority Survey

Upon defining the initial requirements and presenting them to a broader group of stakeholders, I observed a divergence in the perceived importance of some features. Recognizing this, I promptly disseminated a survey to internal employees who are avid sportsbook users. The survey aimed to identify the features they use most frequently and those used least often. They were also encouraged to suggest any missing features. This exercise proved instrumental in understanding which features required prime accessibility and helped us pinpoint elements we could potentially eliminate or reevaluate for their relevance.

Card Sorting

In order to enhance alignment regarding the product vision among stakeholders, I facilitated multiple card sorting workshops involving them.

Key Takeaways

Participants introduced a new category, "My Inventory," for viewing available power-ups, free bets, and promotions.

All groups expressed their anticipation to find recommended bets prominently displayed on the homepage.

There was over an 80% agreement concerning the placement of features on the Homepage, Sports Event View, and within the My Inventory section.

Three out of four groups highlighted their expectation to access casino games from the sports event view, primarily during the live match center interaction.

I personally executed these tasks, noting the ease of completion and number of required steps.

Design & Iterate
I held weekly meetings with the Product Owner to review wireframes and prototypes. I showcased refined designs, highlighting their strengths and weaknesses to stakeholders, and iteratively enhanced them based on the feedback received.

Prototyping played a pivotal role in this project, especially considering the geographically dispersed team across Europe.

Frequently, wireframes were transformed into Lo-Fi and Hi-Fi prototypes using Axure. This not only facilitated a better understanding of the flow and allowed quick testing of different interactions, but it also minimized potential misunderstandings and enabled me to illustrate ideas challenging to verbalize. I often used paper prototypes as well, with a significant breakthrough in the project occurring one evening while sketching on Post-It Notes.

Develop

Reviews

I was deeply engaged in conducting design and animation reviews throughout the entirety of the project. My responsibilities included ensuring the uniformity of visuals and design language across all platforms. Moreover, I took charge of confirming the accurate execution of user flows and UI behaviors. I was readily available to address any queries from developers and designers, and I provided them with supplementary wireframes and prototypes whenever the need arose.

Mobile Web & Desktop

As the project neared the conclusion of its design phase, the business indicated its intention to launch the product on both mobile web and desktop platforms. In response, I formulated a comprehensive responsive layout framework that encompassed multiple breakpoints, catering to a range of devices from compact smartphones to expansive monitors. I skillfully employed various smartphone components to seamlessly adapt the interface to larger screens, all while upholding the integrity of the user experience.
Validate

Internal Testing & Usability Testing

The application was made accessible to Gamesys employees for internal testing, with approximately 600 participants taking part. Additionally, six casual bettors and three advanced bettors were recruited for usability testing sessions.
